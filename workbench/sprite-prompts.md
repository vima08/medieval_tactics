# Промпты спрайтов · imagegen

Использован встроенный imagegen, режим генерации и затем извлечения фона. Оба результата сохранены в `public/sprites/`; выбран `fighters-v2.png`. Проверка alpha выполняется браузерным сценарием `scripts/sprite-browser.mjs`.

## Генерация

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent sprite animation atlas for an original browser isometric low-fantasy tactical game 'Ash and Banner'. Create ONE clean sprite sheet with EXACTLY 4 columns and 6 rows, 24 separate full-body figures, NO text, NO borders, NO scenery, genuinely transparent background. All grid cells equal size. Each figure stays inside its cell, generous transparent margins, feet at the same baseline inside each cell, same consistent scale and orthographic isometric camera looking down slightly, facing screen-right three-quarter. Rows from top to bottom: 1 swordsman with iron sword and short coat, 2 archer with long curved wooden bow and quiver, 3 spearman with long spear, 4 shield bearer with large broad wooden shield, 5 scout with hood and short dagger, 6 engineer with leather apron and hammer. Four columns are actual animation keyframes of SAME character per row: column1 idle ready pose; column2 walk left foot forward; column3 walk right foot forward; column4 forceful attack pose with class weapon extended (archer releases bow). Character identity, costume, weapon and size must stay consistent in all four frames of each row. Premium hand-painted miniature style, crisp simple readable silhouettes, substantial expressive shapes, weathered iron, linen, desaturated sage/olive coats, cream faces, dark brown leather, warm subtle highlights. Lighting upper left, soft restrained shadows on body only. Distinct silhouettes, believable anatomy, no photorealism, no pixel art, no text or extra characters. Exact regular grid is essential for automated cell slicing. Make the sheet portrait ratio 2:3, e.g. 1024x1536, each cell 256x256, each figure occupies about 65% cell width and 80% cell height. Long spear must fit entirely inside cell. Keep weapon tips and all limbs within each cell. Avoid background color/checkerboard, ground shadows, duplicated weapons, UI, labels, cropped figures.

## Извлечение фона

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied sprite atlas. Remove ALL brown background, gradients and floor shadows, make every empty area genuinely transparent with alpha=0. Preserve exactly the 24 character sprites, their fine weapon tips, their painted pixels, their identities, all four columns and six rows, their positions, scale, framing and original 1024x1536 canvas. This must be a game sprite sheet with actual alpha channel, not simulated transparency or checkerboard. No colored background, no checkerboard, no backdrop of any kind. Do not repaint characters. Only extract each sprite cleanly. Retain original grid and pixel dimensions.
